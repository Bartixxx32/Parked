• A spot photo can now be sent explicitly to the family with “Send to family”. The photo is compressed and end-to-end encrypted on the phone.
• The family server stores only ciphertext and deletes it as soon as every family phone downloads the photo, or after 24 hours.
• Received family photos are shown in Settings → Family.
• Family map polling now runs only while the map is visible and the app is in the foreground, saving battery and server quota.
• Same package and signing key — a normal update over 1.4.1.
